Sponsors & partners

The organisations behind the event

DrupalAsia Connect India 2027 brings the Drupal community across Asia and beyond to IIT Bombay for three days: the AI Summit, a conference day of keynotes and workshops, and a day of contribution.

The event is run by volunteers. Sponsorship keeps tickets affordable and funds travel grants for community members across the region. Thank you to every organisation on this page.
