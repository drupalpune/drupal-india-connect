Not sure which ticket to choose?

The Full Combo offers the broadest access. If you are coming mainly for the Drupal conference, Conference Only is the simplest choice. Workshops are booked separately and are not included in any ticket.

Before you register

Good to know

A few details worth checking before you complete your registration.

Does my conference pass also include the AI Summit?

No. Choose AI Summit + Conference or Full Combo for both experiences. Conference Only and Conference + Networking do not include the AI Summit.

Are workshops included in the ticket price?

No. Workshops are booked separately and are not included in any ticket. Workshop booking details will be announced.

TBC
Is networking included with every ticket?

No. Networking is included with the Full Combo and Conference + Networking tickets. Check the ticket inclusions at checkout.

When do Early Bird prices end?

Early Bird prices run until 10 December 2026, 11:59 PM IST, or until sold out. Check KonfHub for the current status.

Where do I receive my ticket?

Registration and checkout are handled on KonfHub. Use your correct attendee details and keep your registration confirmation for event check-in.

Where can I find travel, accommodation and venue information?

The Travel & Stay page covers transport options, accommodation suggestions and visitor guidance. Room details and campus entry instructions will be published closer to the event.

Which event policies apply?

Every attendee agrees to the Code of Conduct. Refund and transfer terms will be published here once confirmed.
