The event

One Drupal community, one continent

DrupalAsia Connect brings the regional camps of Asia into a single shared moment. Instead of a dozen conversations happening in parallel, the region gets one stage — for the agencies scaling Drupal delivery, the teams maintaining modules, the government and university platforms running on it, and the people who want to start.

Expect a programme built from the community: talks and panels, hands-on workshops, contribution sprints, a business track for agency and end-user leadership, and plenty of unstructured time for the conversations that only happen in person.

3 days Sessions, workshops and sprints
Multi-track Development, DevOps, content, business
Pan-Asia Local camps, one regional stage

Why attend

Whether you build with Drupal every day or decide where the budget goes, there is a reason to be in the room.

FAQ

Questions

Something not covered here? Write to hello@drupalasia.org.

Where will DrupalAsia Connect be held?

The host city is being finalised with regional organisers and will be announced along with the venue, hotel partners and travel guidance. Dates are fixed: 18–20 January 2027.

When do tickets go on sale?

Early-bird tickets are released to the mailing list first, shortly after the venue announcement. Pricing will be published at the same time.

Can I speak if I have never presented before?

Yes. A share of the programme is reserved for first-time speakers, and every accepted new speaker is paired with a mentor for rehearsal and feedback.

Will there be travel or ticket grants?

A grant programme funded by sponsors will cover tickets and part of the travel cost for community members who could not otherwise attend. Applications open with the CFP.

Is the event in English?

The main programme is in English. We are exploring local-language tracks and session recordings with subtitles for the region.

Be first to know

Venue, CFP and early-bird tickets — announced to the list before anywhere else. No more than one email a month.
