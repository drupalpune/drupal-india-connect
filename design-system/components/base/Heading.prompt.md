Explicit-control heading: semantic level and visual size are separate props.

```jsx
<Heading text="One Drupal community, one continent" level={2} size="6xl" />
<Heading text="Highlights" level={2} size="xs" style="accented" />
```

`style="accented"` is the system's signature small heading — uppercase, 0.08em tracking, 4px primary left rule. Use it for section eyebrows and stat-block labels.
