Rich-text container. Owns the vertical rhythm for headings, lists, tables, blockquotes and figures inside body copy, and scales responsively per size step.

```jsx
<Text size="lg">Three days of sessions, workshops and contribution sprints.</Text>
```

Use `color="inverted"` on navy and orange bands. Every size step grows at the 700px and 1000px breakpoints — `normal` is 16 → 18 → 20px.
