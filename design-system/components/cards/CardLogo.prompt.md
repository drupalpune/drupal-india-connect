A padded tile holding a partner or sponsor logo, for logo walls on marketing pages.

```jsx
<CardLogo media="assets/sponsors/acme.svg" mediaAlt="Acme" url="https://acme.example" backgroundColor="secondary" borderRadius="medium" />
```

For the real sponsor listing driven by sponsor nodes use `SponsorTeaser` instead — it carries the grayscale-to-colour hover and the tier treatment.
