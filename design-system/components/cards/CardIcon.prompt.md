Value-proposition card: glyph, heading, short description. The "why attend" and "what to expect" grids are built from these.

```jsx
<CardIcon icon="rocket" iconStyle="primary-background" text="Contribute together" description="Mentored sprints where first-time contributors get their first issue credit." textAlign="left" iconAlign="left" backgroundColor="background" borderRadius="medium" />
```

Left-align icon and text for dense four-up grids; centre them for three-up feature rows. Keep descriptions to one or two lines.
