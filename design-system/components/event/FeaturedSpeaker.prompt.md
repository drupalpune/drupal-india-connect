Speaker teaser for the featured-speakers row. Its signature is the duotone portrait: grayscale at 180% contrast over a scroll-filling colour wedge (primary on odd items, secondary on even), colourising on hover.

```jsx
<FeaturedSpeaker name="Dries Buytaert" image="assets/people/dries.jpg" badge="Keynote" body="Founder, Drupal" href="/speakers/dries" />
```

Designed to live on a dark band in rows of three — the wedge animation staggers per position. Portraits must be 1:1 and reasonably tightly cropped.
